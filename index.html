About Aaron

Aaron hails from Atlanta, Georgia. His canon of work spans a diverse range of literary projects. His debut series, Monarch, a unique four-part exploration of an unsettled housewife's impending doomsday fears, sets the tone for his engaging storytelling style.

Aaron's subsequent works venture further into the dark corridors of psychological uncertainty. Psykosis, one such venture, weaves an intriguing narrative around a lawyer entangled in the sinister deeds of a hypnotist. Michaelmas, another of his profound narratives, transports readers to 18th-century England and encapsulates the turbulent transition from an era of magic to the advent of science.

His newest, House Fly, is a novella that chronicles the journey of a recent divorcee unknowingly stepping into a complex commitment in a new town.

Beyond the realm of books, Aaron has lent his unique voice to the spheres of film and television screenwriting. In 2023, he began experimenting with AI tools to create his first illustrated book, a new edition of Michaelmas. No matter the medium, his distinctive style bridges the mundane and the sublime, challenging readers and viewers alike to confront the dichotomy between light and darkness inherent in us all.

House Fly

Part One of a Two-Part Psychological Sci-fi Series · $2.99

In the summer of 2006, Harold Landis, a recent divorcee at a crossroads in his life, moved to Silver City to start all over again. He was lured to the area by a too-good-to-pass-up job opportunity. The multi-billion dollar company that hired him owns most of the town and has set Harold up in a sweet house in a new neighborhood. In between dates that he's lined up for himself online, all he has to do is test products and provide feedback. What could go wrong?

Desperate to get back into the game, Harold wanders into the world of online dating to try and find the right woman. His early efforts are trial and error as he learns the ropes of dating in the 21st century. Things get interesting when a house fly enters his new home and agitates Archie, Harold's playful yet territorial beagle hound.

Michaelmas

Illustrated edition · $2.99

On an autumn evening in 1727, Thomas Edward Downs sits down with his family to celebrate Michaelmas, in memory of the archangel casting Lucifer out of heaven. Their goose dinner is interrupted by the thunderous sound of a large horse-drawn carriage charging up the lane.

Doctor Rathbone, a colleague from the local apothecary, implores Thomas to leave with him at once. His assistance is needed with an urgent situation out at the childhood home of the recently deceased Sir Isaac Newton.

Drawn by duty and curiosity, Thomas kisses his wife and children goodbye and heads out the door and into the rainy night. Upon arriving at Woolsthorpe Manor, the men are ushered inside. Knowing Newton only as the father of modern science, they are unprepared for what they discover in the basement.

Psykosis

$3.99

When the newly appointed District Attorney is summoned to prison by the man he helped condemn to death, he is warned of an unnamed peril that shall soon befall him.

After the execution, the mentally exhausted DA is reluctant to bring his stress and worries back home. He decides to visit a local hypnotherapist's office.

Soon his life is turned upside down. Who he was is no longer who he is. The DA's waking life and his dreams become muddled, and he finds himself mixed up in the activities of a murderous criminal underground. Desperate to find an escape, he begins a search for the mysterious doctor behind it all.

Monarch

A Novel in Four Stages · $4.99

To her suburban friends and neighbors, Ginger Reed, a beautiful housewife who is about to gracefully turn forty, seems to have it all: a nice home within a good school district, two wonderful and healthy children and a handsome husband who supports them. But inside, behind her brilliant green eyes, there flickers a flame of doubt; she believes, with every fiber of her being, that "all is not as it seems," and is afraid that her idyllic world may be coming to an end in less than two months.

The story opens with one month and eleven days until the highly publicized end of the Mayan Calendar. That is why Ginger has driven to a downtown bookstore to buy the book Cipher, a work of fantasy fiction dealing with the approaching date. And that book is the reason why there is such an eclectic assortment of people lined out the door and around the corner to meet the author.

Monarch is the life-changing journey of a woman obsessed with time. A fear of the future has seized the focus of Ginger's mind. She finds herself feeling alone amongst others, fretting over what tomorrow might bring.

Tick-tock, tick-tock, the seconds go flying off the clock.

Now, if only she can finish the book, survive that ominous day, and keep her family and herself together, then maybe she can find her way through this dark psychological tunnel before it's too late.

STORiCORE

STORiCORE is Aaron's multimedia company. It adapts the stories he has written into new forms and builds apps that support storytelling. AI tools help with production, but the writing and every creative decision are his.

Apps

STORiCORE also builds apps, including 1st Draft, a voice-first writing assistant for writers. You'll find them on STORiCORE.com.

How the work gets made

Every story starts with Aaron's own writing. Behind the studio is a production system Aaron built himself. Each story is broken down once into its characters, places and scenes, and that foundation feeds every format. AI does the heavy lifting, and Aaron makes the creative calls at every step. AI-assisted, human-directed.

Helping others do the same

Building STORiCORE taught Aaron what AI can and can't do in real work. He now offers that experience through AI Consulting for writers, creators and small businesses. Tell him what you're trying to do, and he'll tell you plainly whether AI can help and how.
